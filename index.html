      Risk rules

      • Risk 0.5–1% of your account per trade; size from the stop distance (the calculator does this).
      • Low-priced and penny stocks: half the normal risk, limit orders only, never average down.
      • CRWD, NVDA, COIN and HOOD are correlated tech/crypto trades; don't run them all at full size.

      • Close day trades before 4:00pm ET (midnight Dubai).
